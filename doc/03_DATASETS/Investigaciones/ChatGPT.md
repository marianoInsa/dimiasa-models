# Datasets de detección de caídas con IMU

Tras revisar repositorios públicos (PhysioNet, IEEE DataPort, Mendeley) y la literatura reciente (Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar), identificamos varios conjuntos de datos inerciales (3 ejes de acelerómetro ± giroscopio) que complementan al conocido **SisFall**. Los más relevantes son **AybuFall** (Tokgöz & Kocaoglu 2026)[[1]](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13104-026-07679-9#ref-CR19#:~:text=The%20dataset%20comprises%20motion%20recordings,systematically%20organized%20to%20support%20algorithm), el dataset móvil de **Wertner et al.** (2015)[[2]](https://www.know-center.at/filemaker/pdf_publikationen/An%20Open%20Labelled%20Dataset%20for%20Mobile%20Phone%20Sensing%20Based%20Fall%20Detection.pdf#:~:text=contains%20both%20accelerometer%20and%20gy%02roscope,1), **UP-Fall** (Martínez-Villaseñor et al. 2019)[[3]](https://sites.google.com/up.edu.mx/har-up/#:~:text=If%20you%20use%20this%20data,set%2C%20please%20cite%20as%20follows), **KFall** (Yu et al. 2021)[[4]](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/aging-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnagi.2021.692865/full#:~:text=This%20dataset%20was%20generated%20from,Every%20participant%20signed%20informed)[[5]](https://sites.google.com/view/kfalldataset#:~:text=The%20aim%20of%20KFall%20dataset,addition%2C%20for%20each%20fall%20motion), **FallAllD** (Saleh et al. 2021)[[6]](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/343780462_FallAllD_An_Open_Dataset_of_Human_Falls_and_Activities_of_Daily_Living_for_Classical_and_Deep_Learning_Applications#:~:text=...%202%29%20FallAllD%20Dataset%20,)[[7]](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/343780462_FallAllD_An_Open_Dataset_of_Human_Falls_and_Activities_of_Daily_Living_for_Classical_and_Deep_Learning_Applications#:~:text=,60%5D%2C%20which%20includes%20inertial) y **UMAFall** (Casilari et al. 2017)[[8]](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/318385531_UMAFall_A_Multisensor_Dataset_for_the_Research_on_Automatic_Fall_Detection#:~:text=Table%203,selected%20to%20the%20maximum%20value)[[9]](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/318385531_UMAFall_A_Multisensor_Dataset_for_the_Research_on_Automatic_Fall_Detection#:~:text=Fig,iment%20s%20t%20ook%20place). Todos incluyen datos de acelerómetro tri-axial (y mayoría también giroscopio), simulaciones de caídas y ADL, y muchos incorporan “falsos positivos” (ADL que pueden parecer caídas, como sentarse rápido).
