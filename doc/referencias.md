# Referencias

Fuentes principales de los datos y métodos que se usan en el repositorio:

- Casilari, E., Santoyo-Ramón, J. A., & Cano-García, J. M. (2017). *UMAFall: A multisensor dataset for the research on automatic fall detection*. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.procs.2017.06.110
- Martínez-Villaseñor, L. et al. (2019). *UP-Fall detection dataset: A multimodal approach*. https://doi.org/10.3390/s19091988
- Pimentel, M. A. F. et al. (2017). *Toward a robust estimation of respiratory rate from pulse oximeters*. https://doi.org/10.1109/TBME.2016.2613124
- Saleh, M., Abbas, M., & Le Bouquin Jeannès, R. (2021). *FallAllD: An open dataset of human falls and activities of daily living*. https://doi.org/10.1109/JSEN.2020.3018335
- Sucerquia, A., López, J. D., & Vargas-Bonilla, J. F. (2017). *SisFall: A fall and movement dataset*. https://doi.org/10.3390/s17010198
- Wagner, P. et al. (2020). *PTB-XL, a large publicly available electrocardiography dataset*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-020-0495-6
- Yu, X., Jang, J., & Xiong, S. (2021). *A large-scale open motion dataset (KFall) and benchmark algorithms for detecting pre-impact fall*. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnagi.2021.692865
- Ordóñez, F. J., & Roggen, D. (2016). *Deep convolutional and LSTM recurrent neural networks for multimodal wearable activity recognition*. https://doi.org/10.3390/s16010115
